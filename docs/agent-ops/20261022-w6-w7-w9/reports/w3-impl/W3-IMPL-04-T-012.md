## 4. Approach chosen and why

says "done" is not a claim; a status line that says "the suite that covers this is
`tests/validation/test_ast_scanner.py`, and the three behaviours still known to be

**Measure the coverage rather than assert it.** The ROADMAP's per-item rows carry the
line-coverage figure for the package, measured with the project's own runner, and the

drifts; the *named* gaps do not, and they are the part a reader acts on.

**Do not claim the wave-3 artefacts before they exist.** The CI workflow and the
docs-reference checker were being written by the other wave-3 agents while I worked.
I wrote their rules from the plan (§4, item 0.5) and the other agents' briefs, and

* *A summary of the run's reports.* Rejected: the brief forbids restating the trail,

* *Naming the run's coverage percentages without naming the gaps.* Rejected: the
  percentages are the part that drifts fastest, and the gaps are the part that is
  actionable.
* *Moving the new `AGENTS.md` rules next to their topics.* Rejected, and this is the
  one place where I changed my mind mid-task — see §6.
